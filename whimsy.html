How Whimsy sees you

Your camera video is processed entirely on your device, in your browser, using on-device hand tracking. Whimsy never uploads or records your live video.

In AI Transform mode, if you choose to transform a sketch, a single still snapshot of your drawing (not your face or video) is sent to Anthropic's API to generate a creative interpretation. Nothing is sent unless you tap Transform.

Closing the studio, or leaving the page, stops your camera immediately.
